Hello! My name is Ava Espejo, and I’m an undergraduate student here at the University of California, Riverside. I currently study Sociology, but also take a great interest in marketing and graphic design! I’ve had a strong passion for art ever since I was young, and love to find outlets to creatively express myself. Hence, as I pursue my academics and build on my experience and professionalism, I just hope to incorporate my passions with what I’m learning in school while continuing to do what I love! Being a 3rd Year at UCR, I’ve been grateful to already have experienced a handful of past opportunities to use my creativity and work on projects that I can say I’m genuinely proud of. I’ve had the chance to collaborate with others and have been exposed to so many different, but helpful tools and techniques over the past three years. Through feedback from my peers, I’ve been able to refine my work, strengthen my abilities, and overall build both creatively and professionally.
One of the most meaningful experiences that I’ve had here at UCR was being the Outreach PR Director for the Filipino Organization on Campus. Being a part of the Outreach team, I was able to create a variety of materials, whether that be physical merchandise or social media posts and or other forms of content creation. This contributed to our 150+ person event “KE,” otherwise known as Katipunan Experience. Through this event, we were able to invite high school students onto the UCR Campus and show them around and expose them to what college is all about and what it has to offer. To promote this event, I was able to create T-Shirts, Tote Bags, and Stickers, alongside social media posts, flyers, and reels to get people to attend. Throughout this extensive process, I worked closely with my director and fellow team members to make this event come to life. The entire process taught me how important collaboration, communication, time management, and flexibility was when it comes to planning and hosting such a large event. Overall, this was a very positive experience that I hope had a lasting impact on the high schoolers that attended!

Being a Sociology major, I believe I can develop a deeper understanding of human behavior, social relationships, and the overall ways people act on a daily basis within society. Connecting this to Marketing and Graphic Design, two things that I have a deep passion for, I’m hoping to combine my interests and pursue a career in something I truly enjoy and am passionate about postgrad. Moving forward, I hope to keep on learning and be able to step out of my comfort zone whenever I have the opportunity to. I want to be able to meet new people and be exposed to new things in order to build my skills and experience, and just be successful in whatever I pursue! But that is all from me, please do not hesitate to reach out to me here!
